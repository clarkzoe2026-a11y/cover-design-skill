# 配色与提示词

## 品牌色如何进入浅纸风格

以下是可选起点，不是固定品牌规范；用户提供的配色优先。

| 方向 | 纸面 | 字与版画刻线 | 强调 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 浅绿品牌 | #EDF2DF | #26352C | #DDFB78 |
| 工具工作流 | #E1ECEA | #304D49 | 低饱和青绿 |
| 视频表达 | #E3EBF2 | #293F54 | 低饱和雾蓝 |
| 设计方法 | #EDE6DA | #51483B | 浅沙色 |

深色字负责可读，强调色可用于标签或一小块纸片。浅绿字直接放在浅纸上可能对比不足，应改为深字配浅绿底。生成色号是指导值，不能仅凭提示词声称图片每个像素严格符合品牌色。

## 一次真正可用的提示词

本例是建站教程。换主题时，先重写动作隐喻，再组织该主题自己的提示词，不只替换标题。

```text
Create ONE finished 4:3 landscape Chinese video cover.
Art direction: text-first editorial design, engraved / halftone concept collage,
tactile matte paper, soft cut-paper shadows, generous breathing room.
This is an independent cover, not a website screenshot or a device mockup.

EXACT TEXT:
Small topic label: “Coze编程实操”
Secondary line: “网站搭建 | 全流程”
Main headline on two lines: “从买域名” / “到备案”
Supporting line: “买域名 · 备案 · 解析配置 · 上线”
No additional copy. Preserve every Chinese character and the separator.

HIERARCHY:
The main headline is the first thing read at thumbnail size.
Modern, clear bold Chinese sans-serif with natural line breaks and consistent
left alignment. Text takes roughly the left half; illustration is secondary.
Keep 7–8% safe margins and a quiet gap between type and scene.

PALETTE:
Very pale sage paper #EDF2DF across the canvas, deep charcoal-green #26352C
for text and etched lines, a small signature yellow-green #DDFB78 paper accent.
No need for exact pixel color matching claims.

MEANINGFUL SCENE:
A finely engraved hand gives a small house-shaped website its address tag;
an ivory registration paper with a plain checkmark sits behind it, and a small
front gate is open. This expresses acquiring an address and registering a site.
The browser façade is invented, not a personal website. The tag has only an
abstract globe and a blank stroke: no real URL, identity or registration number.
One cohesive paper collage, not a row of isolated workflow icons.

REFERENCES:
Use the supplied image for etched-paper technique and editorial quality only.
Do not copy its title, palette, real-world entities or subject automatically.
```

## 内容变了，图就应变

- 知识库：散落纸页被整理进分类抽屉，再取出使用。
- 静图转视频：一张静止小鸟图变为展翅的连续画面。
- 网站迁移：把同一个网站模型从旧展台移到自有服务器。
- 域名备案：给网站挂上地址牌，再完成登记。

这些是叙事起点，不是预制素材库。不要把抽象“AI”“效率”硬塞进无关机械臂、芯片或机器人。
